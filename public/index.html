/ ai research & experiments

I’m learning how language models work by taking them apart: tokenization, quantization, architecture and training, plus open-weight models running locally through Ollama. Two small experiments below run in your browser.

Quantization

Round 64 synthetic weights to fewer bits and watch the error grow.

Uniform symmetric quantization of a synthetic weight vector. An illustration, not a benchmark.

Tokenization

A tiny byte-pair encoder, trained live in your browser.

Learned from a short built-in text, so common words merge into single tokens and unfamiliar ones fall apart. Real tokenizers train on vastly more data. A dot marks a leading space.
