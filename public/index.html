01About

I build things for headsets, servers and browsers, and I don’t stop shipping.

I’m a developer from Virginia. For the last 4+ years I’ve been pushing updates nonstop: VR games with real player communities, open-source work, and AI projects that run on hardware I own.

I’m a software engineer, a game developer and a full stack developer, depending on what the project needs that week.
